# Code of conduct

Renart contributors and community members are expected to make participation
welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free, regardless of background,
identity, experience level, or role.

Be constructive, assume good intent while addressing impact, respect differing
viewpoints, and focus criticism on the work. Harassment, threats, deliberate
intimidation, discriminatory language, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, or
sustained disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently
exclude participants whose behavior is inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.
Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and
protect the affected community members.

Report conduct concerns privately through
[GitHub's maintainer contact channel](https://github.com/renart-data/renart/security/advisories/new).
Do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue. Reports will
be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy applies in all Renart project spaces and when someone is publicly
representing the project.
